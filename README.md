# Applied AI Engineer — 24-Week Learning Tracker

A self-paced, task-based programme to move from full-stack software development into Applied AI Engineering.

- **Start:** 30 November 2026
- **Target finish:** 16 May 2027
- **Career direction:** Applied AI Engineer (reassess after Weeks 8 and 16)
- **Learning budget:** Free resources
- **Study style:** Flexible sessions whenever time is available, including office breaks
- **Daily target:** Up to 4 hours; progress is task-based, not clock-based
- **Main portfolio project:** [Perfex AI Engineering Agent](https://github.com/jaydev5220/perfex-ai-engineering-agent), maintained separately

## Important separation

This repository is the single source of truth for learning checklists, exercises, notes, progress and weekly reviews. The Perfex AI Engineering Agent repository remains dedicated to product code and product documentation. Do not put learning exercises in the product repository or copy confidential client/employer code here.

## How to learn

1. Open [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) and pick the next unchecked task.
2. Study one small concept using the free resource linked in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
3. Complete the practical exercise independently.
4. Create the specified file or evidence in this repository.
5. Run and verify your work.
6. Commit and push the result.
7. Mark the task complete only after the evidence is pushed.
8. If you have only 15 minutes, make partial progress and record it. Never skip ahead to match a calendar date.

The 7 AM and 10 PM reminders are prompts only. Study whenever you have available time.

## Free resource policy

Prefer official documentation and reputable free courses. No paid course or paid LLM subscription is required. Use local open models and free tools where hardware permits.

## Completion standard

A task is complete only when:
- [ ] You can explain the concept in your own words.
- [ ] The exercise runs and passes its checks.
- [ ] The required artifact is committed and pushed.
- [ ] The relevant checklist and [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) are updated.
- [ ] Remaining questions are recorded.
